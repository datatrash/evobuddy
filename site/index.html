Check a room’s temperature and open its controls from your home screen.

Rooms, schedules
and shortcuts.

Set a temperature.
Choose when it ends.

Drag the slider for larger changes. Keep an override for a duration, until a chosen time, or until you change it.

The visual transition shows which scheduled temperature resumes afterwards.

  • Reorder rooms by dragging
  • Keep favourites within reach
  • Apply the controller’s home modes

Edit the week
in one place.

Choose times with the picker, change temperatures and copy days between rooms. Review the changes before saving.

  • Copy days or whole weeks
  • Import and export room schedules
  • Restore the previous saved schedule

Save a setting
for several rooms.

Store rooms, a temperature and a duration or end time. Edit them later, then run the shortcut from the app, a launcher icon or a widget.

Your existing
Evohome setup.

An Evohome system linked to a European Total Connect Comfort account, an Android 8.0+ phone and an internet connection.

EvoBuddy connects directly to Resideo’s cloud using your TCC account.

No ads.
No analytics.

Your TCC password is used to sign in and is never stored. Session tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore. Room data and shortcuts stay in the app’s private storage.
