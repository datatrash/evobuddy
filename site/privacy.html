Privacy policy

EvoBuddy is an independent Android client for European Evohome systems. It connects directly to Resideo’s Total Connect Comfort service. It has no developer-operated account server, advertising SDK or analytics service.

Signing in and controlling rooms

Your email and password are sent over HTTPS to tccna.resideo.com to sign in. Your password is not saved. The access and refresh tokens needed for later requests are encrypted using a key held by Android Keystore.

To operate the heating system, EvoBuddy sends account authentication, location/system/room identifiers and the temperature, schedule or home-mode changes you request to Resideo. Ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, is also available to that service. Resideo processes these requests under its own terms and privacy statement.

Data on your phone

Room readings, schedules, previous schedule backups, favourites, room order, shortcuts and pending commands are kept in the app’s private storage. Launcher shortcuts and widgets expose the names and readings you choose to place on your home screen. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app.

Disconnecting the account clears its stored data and cancels pending delivery. This does not undo a change already sent to your controller. Uninstalling the app removes its private storage. Schedule files you explicitly export remain in the location you selected.

Background work and permissions

  • Internet and network-state access support direct TCC requests and network-aware retries.
  • Background jobs refresh readings and retry requested changes. Reboot support restores those jobs.
  • A short foreground service applies requested changes, with a silent temporary Android notification or active-app indicator.

EvoBuddy does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or access to shared files. Importing and exporting schedules uses Android’s document picker. It does not upload diagnostics or crash reports to the developer.

This website

This static site uses no cookies, analytics scripts, external fonts or login forms. Netlify hosts the site and processes normal web requests and connection logs under its privacy policy. Following links to GitHub or Resideo takes you to those services.

Questions

Use the issue tracker for questions or corrections. Do not post account credentials, tokens or private heating data.
